SportsOne is an independent sports newsroom. We publish match reports, breaking
news, transfer coverage, analysis and live scores across football, cricket,
tennis, basketball, motorsport, athletics and more.

## What independent means here

No proprietor with a stake in a club, no advertiser deciding what gets covered,
and no tracking of the people who read us. We are answerable to readers and to
the record.

## How we publish

Every story on this site is written in plain Markdown, reviewed, and published
straight from our editorial repository. There is no content management system
between the desk and the page — which means fewer moving parts, faster pages
and nothing to go down on a busy Saturday.

## Corrections

We correct errors openly. If a story is wrong, tell us and we will fix it and
say what changed.

## How to reach us

Story tips, corrections and rights enquiries go through [Contact Us](/contact/).
