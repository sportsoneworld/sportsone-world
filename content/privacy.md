The short version: SportsOne is a static website. It sets no cookies of its
own, runs no analytics and carries no advertising or tracking scripts.

## What we collect

Nothing directly. There is no account to create, no newsletter sign-up and no
comment system, so there is nothing for us to store about you.

## Cookies

SportsOne does not set any cookies. Nothing on this site writes to your browser
storage, and nothing needs your consent, which is why you have not been shown a
cookie banner.

## Our host

The site is served as plain files by our hosting provider. Like every web host,
it processes the request your browser makes — your IP address, the page you
asked for, your browser type and the time — to deliver the page and to protect
the service from abuse. We do not receive that data as a readable log or use it
to build a picture of you.

## Links away from the site

Our share buttons are ordinary links. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click
one, and once you are on X, Facebook, WhatsApp or LinkedIn, that company's
privacy policy applies rather than ours.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about our readers, there is nothing to request,
correct or erase. If that ever changes — if we add a newsletter, for example —
this page will change first.

## Contact

Questions about privacy go to the desk via [Contact Us](/contact/).
