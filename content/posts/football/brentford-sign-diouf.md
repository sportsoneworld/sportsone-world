Brentford have signed the Senegal defender El Hadji Malick Diouf from West Ham
for £40m.

It is a large fee by Brentford's standards and a straightforward one by the
league's. The club's recruitment has for years been built on buying before a
player's price reflects what he is; this is the other kind of signing, the one
you make when the squad needs the certainty more than it needs the margin.

## Elsewhere on deadline week

Manchester United signed the Swedish winger Jusu Bah for £400,000 — a fee small
enough to be a rounding error against the Diouf deal, and a reminder that most
of a transfer window is not conducted at these numbers.

Aston Villa are losing Ollie Watkins, who departs as the club's record Premier
League goalscorer. Unai Emery said Watkins had apologised for refusing to play.

At Crystal Palace, a move for Ordonez was in doubt after a medical.
