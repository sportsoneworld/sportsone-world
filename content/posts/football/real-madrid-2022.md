Real Madrid won their fourteenth European Cup in 2022 having trailed on
aggregate in the last sixteen, the quarter-final and the semi-final, and having
had a clear minority of the ball in the final.

## The pattern

Against Paris Saint-Germain they were 2-0 down on aggregate with half an hour
left; Karim Benzema scored three in seventeen minutes. Against Chelsea they lost
the second leg and went through in extra time. Against Manchester City they were
5-3 down with two minutes of normal time remaining; Rodrygo scored twice in a
minute and Benzema won it from the penalty spot.

## What to make of it

The unsatisfying honest answer is that Madrid were not the best team in the
competition and won it anyway, because knockout football rewards a side that
stays within one goal and has forwards who convert half-chances. Benzema scored
15 in the competition. Thibaut Courtois made nine saves in the final, which
Liverpool lost 1-0 to a Vinícius Júnior goal.

Dominance and elimination are not opposites in this format. Three very good
sides found that out in a single spring.
