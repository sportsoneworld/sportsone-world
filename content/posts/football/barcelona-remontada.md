Barcelona lost the first leg of their 2017 Champions League tie 4-0 in Paris.
The second leg at the Camp Nou was, by any reasonable reading, a formality.

## The last seven minutes

Barcelona led 3-1 with two minutes of normal time left, which was not enough.
Neymar scored a free kick on 88 minutes and a penalty on 91. Sergi Roberto, a
converted midfielder playing at right back, scored the sixth in the ninety-fifth
from a Neymar chip.

## Why it is genuinely singular

Sixteen sides had previously lost a European Cup tie's first leg by four goals.
All sixteen went out. It has not been done since either, which suggests the
result was not the start of a pattern so much as an outlier that everybody
subsequently over-learned from.

Paris Saint-Germain's response was to spend €222m on Neymar that summer. Their
reasoning was that they had been eliminated by an individual rather than by a
team, and the fastest correction was to buy the individual. It is a reading of
the night that the ninety-fifth minute does not entirely support.
