England were bowled out for 67 in their first innings of the third Ashes Test at
Headingley in August 2019. It remains one of the lowest totals in modern English
Test cricket, and it should have settled the match.

## The chase

Set 359, England were 286 for nine when Jack Leach joined Ben Stokes. What
followed was not a partnership in any conventional sense. Leach faced 17 balls
and scored one run. Stokes scored 74 of the last 76, finishing 135 not out with
eight sixes, and England won by one wicket to level the series.

## Why it still reads strangely

Because almost nothing about it was repeatable. Stokes went 73 balls without
scoring at one point in the innings, then hit a phase of ball-striking that no
model of Test batting accounts for. Australia had a review left when Leach was
struck on the pad on 358; they had used it two balls earlier on a caught-behind
appeal that was not out.

Series are usually decided by the accumulation of small advantages. This one
turned on an afternoon that refused to behave that way, which is why it is
still argued about seven years later.
