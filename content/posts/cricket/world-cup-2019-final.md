New Zealand made 241 for eight. England made 241 all out. The super over
produced 15 apiece. England were given the World Cup because they had hit more
boundaries across the day, 26 to 17.

## The passages that made it

Ben Stokes finished 84 not out and was at the centre of the two moments that
decided it: an overthrow off his bat that ran to the boundary for six runs, and
a last-over sequence that left England needing 15 from four balls. Trent Boult
caught Stokes at long-on and stepped on the rope. Martin Guptill was run out by
inches at the end of the super over.

## What came after

The International Cricket Council removed the boundary countback within a year.
A tied super over is now followed by another super over, repeated until there is
a winner, which is what most people watching that afternoon had assumed was
already the case.

It is the only World Cup final that has produced two ties and a champion, and
the only one whose result depended on a rule almost nobody could have quoted
before the day began.
