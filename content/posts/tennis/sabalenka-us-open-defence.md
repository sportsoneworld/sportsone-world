Aryna Sabalenka has won the last two US Opens. She arrives to defend the third
having gone since the beginning of April without reaching a final, on a record
of 17 wins and seven losses.

The number that will worry her more is a smaller one. In the third round at
Cincinnati she led Sára Bejlek 5-1 in the first-set tie-break, and 4-1 in the
second set, and lost in straight sets. Under any ordinary reading of a world
number one against an unheralded opponent, that match does not get away.

## What the losing looks like

It is not the errors on their own. It is the indecision in front of them — the
half-second of choosing that was not there eighteen months ago, and the tension
that follows it into the next point. Sabalenka has had bad weeks before. She
has not had a five-month stretch where the tennis looked like a question she
was asking rather than an answer she was giving.

## The players waiting for her

Coco Gauff arrives in the opposite condition. She beat Jessica Pegula in the
Cincinnati final for her first title of 2026, timed as well as a title can be
timed. Arthur Fils won the men's event at the same tournament.

Flushing Meadows has been Sabalenka's best surface and her best fortnight two
years running. It is now also her last chance of a major this season, which is
not the frame of mind anyone would choose for a title defence.
