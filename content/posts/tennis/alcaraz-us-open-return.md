Carlos Alcaraz will defend the US Open having not played a competitive match
for four months. He opens against Roman Safiullin, and the draw does not get
easier after that.

The wrist injury that kept him out since the spring is the whole story of his
tournament. A defending champion arriving without match practice is one
problem; a defending champion arriving without match practice into a section
that offers no gentle first week is another.

## The field has thinned around him

Jannik Sinner, the world number one, withdrew injured. So did Britain's Jack
Draper. Two of the players who would have been expected to stand between
Alcaraz and a second consecutive title are not in New York at all, which makes
the men's draw the most open it has been at a major in some time — and makes
the fitness of the man holding the trophy the most interesting question in it.

## The undercard has its own draw

Stan Wawrinka was given a late wildcard for what will be the final Grand Slam
of his career, having initially not received one. Serena and Venus Williams
were given a doubles wildcard, and will play a major together for the first
time in four years.

Karolina Muchova and Jakub Mensik have already won something here: the mixed
doubles title, and the million dollars that came with it, saved from two match
points on the way.
