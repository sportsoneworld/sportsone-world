Shingo Kunieda retired in January 2023 with twenty-eight Grand Slam singles
titles and four Paralympic gold medals, having completed a career Golden Slam.
No wheelchair player has won more.

## What changed around him

When Kunieda started, wheelchair events at the majors were played on outside
courts with no broadcast and, at some tournaments, no draw sheet in the printed
programme. By the time he finished, wheelchair finals were being played on show
courts at all four majors and carried live.

Some of that was administrative. Most of it was that he was, plainly, one of the
best players at whichever tournament he entered, and that became difficult to
schedule around.

## The unfinished part

Prize money remains a fraction of the able-bodied draws. Draw sizes are still
small enough that a player outside the top eight has limited access to the
majors, which constrains how many careers the sport can support.

The audience arrived. The structure underneath it has moved more slowly, and
that is the argument the tour is having now.
