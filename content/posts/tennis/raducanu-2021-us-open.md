Emma Raducanu was ranked outside the top 100 and entered the 2021 US Open
through qualifying. She won ten matches in nineteen days and did not lose a set
in any of them, beating Leylah Fernandez in the final.

## Why it had not happened

Qualifiers had reached major finals before. None had won one, and none had come
through a fortnight of best-of-three tennis without a single set going against
them. Physically it is the harder route by three matches; competitively it means
playing your first main-draw match with no ranking protection and no seeding for
the rest of the draw.

## What followed

Very little of it repeated. Raducanu spent the following seasons dealing with
injuries and a rapid turnover of coaches, and did not go past the second week of
a major again for some time.

That is worth stating plainly rather than treating as a failure. What she did in
New York was an outlier by definition — the kind of fortnight the sport produces
perhaps once a generation, and which nobody, including the player, should be
expected to reproduce on demand.
