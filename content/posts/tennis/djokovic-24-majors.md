Novak Djokovic reached twenty-four Grand Slam singles titles at the 2023 US
Open. The explanations offered at the time were mostly physical: the movement,
the sliding, the recovery from defensive positions.

## What the record actually rests on

Return games. Djokovic has led the tour in return-games-won for most of his
career, and the gap is widest on second serve. A player who wins forty-five per
cent of second-serve return points does not need to break often, because he
converts pressure into breaks at the moments other players are protecting a
lead.

The serve was never a weapon in the way Federer's or Sampras's was. It did not
have to be. A player who holds at a normal rate and returns at an exceptional
one wins the same number of matches as a player who does the reverse, and loses
fewer of them in five sets.

## The part that is not measurable

He also played the longest peak of the three, which is partly training and diet
and partly the ordinary luck of avoiding a career-altering injury. Records at
this level are always a compound of a skill and a run of good fortune. It is
usually the skill that gets discussed.
