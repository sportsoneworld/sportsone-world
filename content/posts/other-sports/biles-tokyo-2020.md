Simone Biles withdrew from the team final at the Tokyo Olympics after one vault,
and subsequently from four individual finals. The explanation she gave was
specific rather than general: she had the twisties, a loss of air awareness in
which a gymnast cannot reliably tell where they are during a rotation.

## Why that is a safety issue, not a nerves issue

A twisting double somersault is performed blind. The gymnast relies on
proprioception to know when to open out for the landing. A gymnast who loses it
mid-air on a vault is descending head-first from about three metres with no
reliable way to correct.

Several gymnasts have ended careers with spinal injuries in precisely that
situation. Biles competed in the beam final, having removed the twisting
element, and won bronze.

## What changed

Every major federation has since written air-awareness protocols into its
medical guidance, and the language around athlete withdrawal has shifted
noticeably.

Biles returned to competition in 2023 and won three gold medals at the Paris
Olympics the following year. Both halves of that sequence matter. The first made
the second possible.
