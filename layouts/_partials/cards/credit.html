WHERE THE NAME COMES FROM, in order: 1. `imageSource` in the article — always use this if you know it: imageSource: "Getty Images" 2. If none of those produce a name, nothing is printed at all — a photograph the desk took itself simply has no line under it. THE FULL, LEGAL ATTRIBUTION — photographer and licence — is not deleted.
